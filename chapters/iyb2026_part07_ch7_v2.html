MoSPI: National Statistical Commission & National Statistics Office

Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) — became an independent ministry in 1999 (merger of Dept. of Statistics + Dept. of Programme Implementation). Two wings: Statistics Wing and Programme Implementation Wing. Autonomous body: Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) — institute of national importance under the ISI Act, 1959.

National Statistical Commission (NSC) — set up 2005, following the Rangarajan Commission recommendation (2001 review); constituted 2006. Evolves/monitors/enforces statistical priorities & standards; has a part-time Chairperson + 4 part-time members; CEO NITI Aayog is an ex-officio member; Secretary MoSPI is Secretary to the Commission.

National Statistics Office (NSO) — coordinates statistical activities & standards; compiles national accounts, IIP, CPI (Urban/Rural/Combined), human development & gender statistics, energy/social/environment statistics, National Industrial Classification.

Word Bank
Ex-officio member
— someone who is automatically a member of a body because of another office they hold, not through separate appointment/election.

National Accounts & Price Statistics

National Accounts: MoSPI prepares GDP, National Income, Govt/Private Final Consumption Expenditure, capital formation & saving estimates — published annually as "National Accounts Statistics." Also releases the annual Supply and Use Table (SUT) (industry-product level snapshot).

Price Statistics: CPI (rural/urban/combined) released monthly, Base Year 2012=100; item basket derived from the Consumer Expenditure Survey (CES) 2011-12. Consumer Food Price Index (CFPI) = weighted average of 10 sub-groups (cereals, meat/fish, egg, milk, oils/fats, fruits, vegetables, pulses, sugar, spices); data from 1,114 urban markets (310 towns) + 1,181 rural markets. CPI base updation currently underway following back-to-back Household Consumption Expenditure Surveys (2022-23 & 2023-24).

International Comparison of Prices & Index of Industrial Production

ICP (International Comparison of Prices): largest global statistical initiative under UNSD; World Bank = global implementing agency, Asian Development Bank = regional (Asia-Pacific) agency; compares GDP using Purchasing Power Parity (PPP); NSO = India's nodal agency. Per ICP 2021 results, India is the 3rd largest economy in the world (PPP-GDP) and 2nd largest in its region; ICP 2024 is in progress.

IIP (Index of Industrial Production): compiled by NSO from 14 source agencies; released on the 28th of every month (per IMF Special Data Dissemination Standards); covers mining/manufacturing/electricity + use-based classification (primary, capital, intermediate, infra/construction, consumer durables/non-durables goods). DPIIT is the major data source — 322 of 407 item groups, weight 47.54% of overall IIP.

Exam angle: CPI base year = 2012; IIP base year is different (2011-12) — a common confusion point between the two indices.

Environment & Multi-Domain Statistics

EnviStats India: Environment Statistics — annual publication since 2018, per UNSD's Framework for Development of Environment Statistics (FDES) 2013; covers 6 components: environmental conditions/quality, resources & use, residuals, extreme events/disasters, human settlements/health, environmental protection/management. MoSPI also compiles Environment Accounts via the UN SEEA Framework.

Multi-Domain Statistics: MoSPI coordinates gender/population/food security/health/education statistics; publishes "Women and Men in India" annually; India is a member of the Inter-Agency and Expert Group on Gender Statistics (IAEG-GS); also coordinates BRICS-related statistical activities.

Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

UN General Assembly's 70th Session (2015) adopted "Transforming our World: the 2030 Agenda" — 17 SDGs + 169 targets (2015-2030), in force from 2016, building on the earlier Millennium Development Goals (MDGs). NITI Aayog = nodal institution coordinating India's SDG efforts; MoSPI develops/reviews the National Indicator Framework (NIF) for monitoring SDGs at national level.

Capacity Development Scheme

Capacity Development (CD) Scheme — ongoing Central Sector Scheme; 2 sub-schemes: Support for Statistical Strengthening (SSS) and Economic Census (EC). Activities: GDP estimates, CPI, IIP support, socioeconomic surveys, statistical coordination, IT infrastructure.

SSS Sub-Scheme — implemented via State Directorates/Bureaus of Economics & Statistics (DESs); strengthens participation in National Sample Survey rounds, GSDP estimation, state-level CPI/IIP/SDG indicators.

Research, Analytics & NSSTA

MoSPI's Research and Analysis Division (RAD) + Data Innovation (DI) Lab support research/analytics and startup/academia collaboration. India is a member of the UN Expert Committee on Big Data & Data Science, with a seat on the Advisory Board of UNCEBD; also contributes to UNECE and holds a position in Paris21. Published the "Compendium of Datasets and Registries in India, 2024" — 270 datasets from 40 ministries.

NSSTA (National Statistical Systems Training Academy) — Central Training Institute under MoSPI for official statistics training, including for other services and developing countries.

Non-Conventional Data Sources: MoSPI incorporates geospatial, mobile-phone, and scanner data; introduced the National Metadata Structure (NMDS) and Statistical Quality Assessment Framework (SQAF).

National Sample Survey (NSS)

Conducting large-scale sample surveys since 1950 — covers Household Consumption/Expenditure, Employment/Unemployment, Health/Education, Migration/Housing, Enterprise Surveys, Debt/Investment, Digital Access/ICT, Crop Statistics. Key surveys: PLFS (Periodic Labour Force Survey), ASUSE (Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises).

Recent reforms: survey results now released within 3 months of completion (per Advance Release Calendar); new high-frequency Comprehensive Modular Survey (CMS); district-level estimates from Jan 2025; PLFS 2025 now generates monthly (not just quarterly) labour indicators; NSS restructured from process-based to product-based; all surveys now use CAPI (Computer Assisted Personal Interview) with real-time validation.

NSS Diamond Jubilee (75 years): National Kick-Off Event, 7 Feb 2025, Vigyan Bhawan; a ₹5 Customised My Stamp and a ₹75 Commemorative Coin released on Statistics Day 2025.

Word Bank
CAPI (Computer Assisted Personal Interview)
— survey data collection done directly on a digital device (tablet/handheld) with built-in validation, instead of paper forms.
District as a basic stratum
— designing a survey's sample so that each district is treated as its own separate sub-group, allowing district-level (not just state/national) estimates.

Statistics Day & India in UN Statistical Commission

Statistics Day — 29 June every year, marking the birth anniversary of (Late) Prof. Prasanta Chandra Mahalanobis, in recognition of his contribution to economic planning & statistical development.

UN Statistical Commission (StatCom) — established 1946; highest body of the global statistical system. India elected as a member for 2024-27, after a gap of ~2 decades. India also re-elected to the Governing Council of the Statistical Institute for Asia and the Pacific (SIAP) for 2025-2027 (previously served 2013-16, 2016-19, 2019-22, 2022-24).

Infrastructure & Project Monitoring

MoSPI's mandate: (i) monitor projects ≥₹150 crore, (ii) monitor infrastructure sector performance, (iii) monitor the Twenty Point Programme.

Project Monitoring: via the PAIMANA portal (Project Assessment, Infrastructure Monitoring and Analytics for Nation-building), integrated with DPIIT's IPMP (Integrated Project Monitoring Portal), following "one data, one entry"; generates monthly Flash Reports.

Infrastructure Performance Monitoring: monthly reports for power, coal, steel, cement, railways, shipping/ports, fertilisers, petroleum/natural gas, civil aviation, roads, telecommunication.

Twenty Point Programme (TPP): launched 1975; restructured as TPP-2006, operational from 1 April 2007 — covers poverty alleviation, employment, housing, education, health, environment; 20 points comprising 65 items across various ministries.

Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS)

Launched 1993 — fully central-funded Central Sector Scheme letting MPs recommend developmental works (drinking water, education, health, sanitation, roads) in their constituencies. Annual entitlement: ₹5 crore per MP/constituency. MPs only recommend; district authorities sanction/execute/complete works. Implementation via the eSAKSHI Portal and mobile app since FY 2023-24; shifted to PFMS Model 1A (TSA Hybrid) from FY 2025-26.
