Neighbourhood First

India's approach to neighbours is driven by Samman (respect), Samvad (dialogue), Shanti (peace), Samriddhi (prosperity).

Word Bank
Persona Non Grata
— a diplomatic term for a foreign official declared unwelcome/unacceptable by the host country, effectively expelling them.
Line of Credit (LoC)
— a loan facility one country extends to another, typically for infrastructure projects, repayable over time.

United States & Russia

USA: "Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership" — cooperation via 2+2 Ministerial, G20, I2U2, Quad. PM's Feb 2025 US visit (first after Trump's 2nd-term inauguration) launched "Mission 500" — double bilateral trade to USD 500 billion by 2030. US VP JD Vance hosted in New Delhi, Apr 2025. India-US MoU on cybercrime cooperation, Jan 2025.

Russia: "Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership" — defence, security, trade, S&T cooperation. 25th & 26th Sessions of IRIGC-TEC held in 2025; EAM's Aug 2025 visit included meeting President Putin; discussed new Indian consulates in Kazan & Yekaterinburg.

Europe & the European Union

France: India–France Horizon 2047 Roadmap; PM co-chaired AI Action Summit with French President, Feb 2025 — signed the India-France Declaration on AI 2025; India-France Year of Innovation planned for 2026.

United Kingdom: India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) signed Jul 2025 — elevates the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership; adopted India-UK Vision 2035; Consulate General opened in Manchester, Mar 2025.

EU: 2nd India-EU Trade and Technology Council (TTC) meeting, Feb 2025 (AI, semiconductors, DPI cooperation); 1st India-EU Strategic Dialogue, Jun 2025.

Other notable visits: President Murmu's State Visit to Portugal & Slovak Republic (Apr 2025, 1st Indian Presidential visit to each in 27 & 29 years respectively); Belgium's Economic Mission led by Princess Astrid (325+ delegates); Germany's 25th Strategic Partnership anniversary logo launch, May 2025.

Japan & Australia

Japan: "Special Strategic and Global Partnership" — ties date to the 6th century. India-Japan Economic Forum, Tokyo, Aug 2025 — PM outlined 5 priority collaboration areas (manufacturing, tech/innovation, green energy, next-gen infrastructure, skill development). Japan-India Semiconductor Supply Chain Partnership reaffirmed.

Australia: "Comprehensive Strategic Partners" — cooperation in migration/mobility and critical/rare earth minerals. India's 4th consulate opened in Brisbane, Nov 2024. 7th India-Australia Disarmament/Non-Proliferation Dialogue, Aug 2025.

West Asia

Part of India's "Look West" policy — energy security, trade, large diaspora. PM's Dec 2024 Kuwait visit — first by an Indian PM in 43 years. 15th India-UAE Joint Commission Meeting; UAE's Deputy PM/FM's first India visit. PM's State Visit to Saudi Arabia, Apr 2025 — co-chaired 2nd India-Saudi Arabia Strategic Partnership Council. Qatar's Amir made a State Visit, Feb 2025 — signed the "Agreement on Establishment of Bilateral Strategic Partnership." 19th round of India-Iran FOC discussed Chabahar Port development; 2025 marked the 75th anniversary of the India-Iran Friendship Treaty.

East/South Africa: PM's State Visit to Namibia, Jul 2025 — first in 27 years; Namibia joined CDRI, GBA, and became first country to adopt UPI via licensing agreement. India thanked for support in the Cheetah conservation project.

Central/West Africa: PM's Nov 2024 Nigeria visit — first by an Indian PM in 17 years. President of Angola's State Visit, May 2025 — first in 38+ years. PM's State Visit to Ghana, Jul 2025 (first in 3 decades) — became first Indian PM to address Ghana's Parliament.

Indian Ocean Region

PM's Mauritius visit, Mar 2025 — 8 MoUs signed; adopted "Joint Vision for an Enhanced Strategic Partnership" and launched Vision MAHASAGAR. 24th Indian Ocean Rim Association Council of Ministers Meeting hosted virtually by Sri Lanka, May 2025. Fiji's PM visited Aug 2025 — welcomed Fiji's membership of the International Solar Alliance, CDRI, GBA; MoU for a 100-bed Super Speciality Hospital in Suva (India's largest Grant-in-Aid project in the Pacific).

Southeast Asia & the Indo-Pacific

Under the Act East Policy. Singapore's President's State Visit, Jan 2025 — marked 60th anniversary of bilateral ties (elevated to Comprehensive Strategic Partnership in Sep 2024). PM's Apr 2025 Thailand visit — India-Thailand Strategic Partnership declared; participated in the 6th BIMSTEC Summit. New Zealand PM's first visit, Mar 2025 — Defence Cooperation MoU signed; NZ joined CDRI.

East Asia: China & Republic of Korea

China: Relations centre on the LAC boundary question and border peace/tranquillity — disturbed by China's April-May 2020 unilateral status-quo change attempts. 2025 marked the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations. PM met President Xi at the SCO Summit, Tianjin (Aug-Sep 2025) — noted successful disengagement. Chinese FM Wang Yi co-chaired the 24th Special Representatives' Dialogue on the Boundary Question; India raised concerns over China's mega dam on the Yarlung Tsangpo (Brahmaputra). Kailash Mansarovar Yatra resumption welcomed.

Republic of Korea: "Special Strategic Partnership" — technology, trade, security focus; new Korean FM's first India visit, Aug 2025.

Exam angle: The India-China LAC standoff began with China's status-quo change attempts in April-May 2020 — a frequently tested date.

Eurasia & Central Asia

Engagement via UN, BRICS, SCO, G20, CICA. Armenia became the 104th full member of the International Solar Alliance, Nov 2024. 4th Meeting of the India-Central Asia Dialogue held Jun 2025 (Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan FMs); PM invited all Central Asian leaders for the 2nd India-Central Asia Summit to be held in India.

Latin America & the Caribbean

PM's Guyana visit, Nov 2024 — participated in 2nd India-CARICOM Summit; received Dominica's highest national honour. PM's Jul 2025 Brazil State visit — targeted increasing bilateral trade to USD 20 billion in 5 years; discussed expanding the India-MERCOSUR Preferential Trade Agreement; 6 MoUs signed (counter-terrorism, agri research, renewable energy, IPR, digital/India Stack). PM's Argentina visit, Jul 2025 — first in 57 years; 2025 marks 75 years of India-Argentina ties. Trinidad and Tobago became first Caribbean nation to adopt UPI.

India remains the largest cumulative contributor to UN Peacekeeping. ~180 Indian peacekeepers have died in service. First tranche of 4 projects under the "India-UN Global Capacity Building Initiative" launched, Aug 2025. Feb 2025: hosted the "Conference on Women Peacekeepers from the Global South" (35 nations) at Manekshaw Centre, New Delhi.

Climate Change

India actively engages under the UNFCCC. Participated in COP 29, Baku, Azerbaijan, Nov 2024 — co-chaired the Leadership Group for Industry Transition (LeadIT) Annual Summit with Sweden, focused on low-carbon steel/cement industrial transition, aligned with the Paris Agreement.

Multilateral Engagements

G20: EAM at G20 FM Meeting, Johannesburg, Feb 2025 — called for UN Security Council reform.

BRICS: PM at the 17th BRICS Summit, Rio de Janeiro, Jul 2025 — reframed the acronym as "Building Resilience and Innovation for Cooperation and Sustainability" under India's upcoming presidency framing.

SCO: PM at the 25th SCO Council of Heads of State, Tianjin, Aug-Sep 2025 — adopted the Tianjin Declaration; reaffirmed support for Chabahar Port & International North-South Transport Corridor.

QUAD: Dec 2024 — Joint Statement marking 20th Anniversary of Quad Cooperation; India hosted the "Quad Workshop on Pandemic Preparedness for the Indo-Pacific," Mar 2025 (during India's Quad presidency).

BIMSTEC: PM at the 6th BIMSTEC Summit, Thailand, Apr 2025; India hosted the 1st BIMSTEC Youth Summit (Gandhinagar, Feb 2025), 2nd BIMSTEC Ports Conclave (Visakhapatnam), 4th Disaster Management Exercise (virtual).

Commonwealth: Ms. Shirley Ayorkor Botchwey visited India, Mar 2025, before becoming the 7th Secretary-General of the Commonwealth, Apr 2025.

Nalanda University

Established via the Nalanda University Act, 2010 at Rajgir, Bihar — envisioned at the East Asia Summits of 2007 & 2009; supported by 18 participating countries. New Net-Zero Green Campus inaugurated 19 June 2024. Offers Masters/PhD in Buddhist studies, historical studies, ecology, management, languages & international relations. As of Aug 2025: 630+ students from 20+ countries.

Counter-Terrorism, Disarmament & Cyber Diplomacy

Counter-Terrorism: After the Pahalgam terror attack (Apr 2025), India sent 7 all-party delegations to 33 countries (May-Jun 2025) to brief on Operation Sindoor and reaffirm zero-tolerance against cross-border terrorism.

Disarmament: Feb 2025 — India + UN Office for Disarmament Affairs held the first-ever Capacity Building Programme on UNSC Resolution 1540 & Strategic Trade Controls (Palasamudram, AP) for 8 Asia-Pacific countries.

Cyber Diplomacy: Cyber Diplomacy Division = MEA's nodal point for cybersecurity/data protection/internet governance. India-US MoU on cybercrime cooperation (17 Jan 2025); 8th India-EU Cyber Dialogue, Mar 2025.

Economic Diplomacy & Development Partnership

Economic Diplomacy (ED) Division — MEA's economic arm; manages ISA, CDRI, I2U2, Social Security Agreements, investment treaties, INR trade settlement & UPI internationalisation.

Development Partnership examples: first trilateral power transaction Nepal→Bangladesh via Indian grid; Jaipur Foot camp in Mozambique (1,200 beneficiaries); 5,000 solar rooftop units in Sri Lanka; High Impact Community Development Projects Phase III in Maldives; broad-gauge railway with Nepal; solar projects in Comoros, Fiji, Madagascar, Seychelles.

Humanitarian Aid & Assistance

Operation Brahma (Myanmar earthquake, Mar 2025) — 16 tonnes HADR material by C-130J; 20 pre-fab offices (~50 tonnes) by C-17.

Operation Sindhu (Jun 2025, Iran-Israel conflict) — evacuated 4,415 Indian nationals (3,597 from Iran, 818 from Israel) via 19 special flights; also evacuated 14 OCI cardholders, 9 Nepali, 4 Sri Lankan nationals.

Other aid: 2,200 MT rice to Myanmar; 1,000 MT rice to Lesotho; USD 500,000 to quake-hit Vanuatu; 26 tonnes aid to Honduras (Storm SARA); 75 Indians evacuated from Syria (Dec 2024, incl. 44 "zaireen" pilgrims from J&K).

Track 1.5 Dialogues & Overseas Indian Affairs

Track 1.5 Dialogues: 10th Raisina Dialogue, New Delhi, Mar 2025 (theme: "Kālachakra - People, Peace and Planet," 125 countries); inaugural Raisina Mediterranean Dialogue, Marseille, Jun 2025; 8th Indian Ocean Conference, Oman; 9th Asia Economic Dialogue, Pune.

Overseas Indian Affairs: 1.46 crore passports issued in 2024. Pre-Departure Orientation Training (PDOT), since Jan 2018 (Pravasi Kaushal Vikas Yojana) — 1,72,220 workers trained till Dec 2024, covering safety schemes like ICWF, MADAD, PBBY. 283 Indian nationals repatriated from Myanmar-Thailand border scam centres, Mar 2025.

E-Governance: e-Migrate (since 2015) — paperless recruitment for ECR emigrants; eSEWA — online consular services; MADAD — consular grievance redressal portal.

Word Bank
Track 1.5 Dialogue
— a diplomatic forum mixing government officials (Track 1) with non-government experts/academics (Track 2), allowing freer discussion than formal state-to-state talks.
ECR (Emigration Check Required)
— countries (mostly Gulf) where certain categories of Indian workers need government emigration clearance before departing for jobs, to prevent exploitation.
OCI cardholder
— Overseas Citizen of India — a status giving persons of Indian origin lifelong visa-free travel and most rights of an Indian citizen (except voting/holding public office), without dual citizenship.
