Department of Commerce & India's International Trade

Department of Commerce (Ministry of Commerce & Industry) formulates/implements the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP); handles multilateral/bilateral trade relations, SEZs, export promotion.

Overall Trade (2024-25): Global trade (goods+services) = USD 1,745.16 billion; total exports USD 825.25 bn (+6.04% YoY); total imports USD 919.92 bn.

Merchandise Trade: India ranked 18th globally in merchandise exports (1.8% share, WTO GTO Apr 2025); exports USD 437.70 billion. Top export categories: Petroleum Products (14.48%), Telecom Instruments (5.96%), Drug Formulations (5.52%). Top destinations: USA (19.77%), UAE (8.37%), Netherlands (5.2%). Electronics exports leapt 32.46% to USD 38.56 billion (smartphones = 62.5% of this). India ranked 9th in merchandise imports (2.8% share); imports USD 721.20 billion; top sources: China (15.73%), Russia (8.85%), UAE (8.79%).

Services Trade: India ranked 8th in services exports (USD 387.54 billion, +13.63% YoY, 4.3% global share) and 9th in services imports (USD 198.72 billion, 3.4% share) — maintains a steady services trade surplus.

Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)

Attached office of the Department of Commerce; HQ at Vanijya Bhavan, New Delhi; 4 Zonal Offices (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) + 24 Regional Authorities. Formulates & implements the Foreign Trade Policy; role has shifted from "prohibition/control" to "facilitator" of exports/imports.

Directorate General of Trade Remedies (DGTR) & DGCIS

DGTR — formed 1997 as DGAD (Anti-Dumping & Allied Duties), restructured as DGTR in May 2018, merging Anti-Dumping (ADD), Countervailing Duty (CVD), Safeguards Duty (SGD) and Safeguards (QR) functions into a single window. India's sole authority for all trade remedial measures; investigations are quasi-judicial.

DGCIS (Directorate General of Commercial Intelligence and Statistics) — collects/compiles/disseminates India's trade statistics (merchandise, services, state/district-wise exports).

Word Bank
Anti-Dumping Duty
— an extra import tax imposed when a foreign exporter sells goods below their normal home-market price ("dumping"), harming domestic industry.
Countervailing Duty
— a duty imposed to offset the effect of a foreign government's subsidy to its exporters.

Export Inspection Council, IIFT & IIP

Export Inspection Council (EIC) — set up under Section 3, Export (Quality Control and Inspection) Act 1963; ensures exported products meet importing-country quality/safety standards. Executed via Export Inspection Agencies (EIAs) at Mumbai, Kolkata, Kochi, Delhi, Chennai + 24 sub-offices (ISO/NABL-accredited labs).

IIFT (Indian Institute of Foreign Trade) — established 1963, Deemed University since 2002; HQ Delhi, campuses in Kolkata, Kakinada, GIFT City; AACSB-accredited (Nov 2021); NAAC Grade A+ (Dec 2023).

IIP (Indian Institute of Packaging) — autonomous body, set up May 1966 under Societies Registration Act 1860; HQ Mumbai; member of World Packaging Organisation & founder member of Asian Packaging Federation.

Government e-Marketplace (GeM)

Set up 2016 as the National Public Procurement Portal — 3 pillars: inclusion, transparency, efficiency. GMV grew from ₹422 crore (FY 2016-17) to ₹15 lakh crore (as of 20 Aug 2025).

Commodity Boards

5 statutory Commodity Boards under the Department of Commerce, responsible for production, development & export of the plantation sector — tea, coffee, rubber, spices, tobacco. Implement central sector schemes for new planting, replanting, quality upgradation, value addition and export promotion.

APEDA & MPEDA

APEDA (Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority) — established under the APEDA Act, 1985; head office + 16 regional offices; covers scheduled products (fruits, vegetables, meat, dairy, cereals, floriculture, cashew, etc.). Protects the GI of Basmati Rice; monitors sugar imports; Secretariat to the National Accreditation Board for NPOP (National Programme for Organic Production).

MPEDA (Marine Products Export Development Authority) — constituted 1972; HQ Kochi, Kerala; 30 members; 18 Field Offices; 3 Trade Promotion Offices (New York, Japan, New Delhi); 5 Quality Control Labs. Set up 3 societies: RGCA (aquaculture technology transfer), NETFISH (fish quality/sustainable fishing), NaCSA (sustainable aquaculture practices).

Special Economic Zones (SEZ)

SEZ Act, 2005 — in force from 10 February 2006; enables single-window clearance. Objectives: additional economic activity, export promotion, investment, employment, infrastructure.

As of 31 Mar 2025: 368 notified SEZs, 276 operational, hosting 6,695 approved units, cumulative investment ₹7,82,192 crore, employment for 31,77,893 persons. FY 2024-25 SEZ exports: ₹14,63,669 crore (~USD 173.07 billion), an 8% YoY increase — ~21% of India's total exports.

ITPO & Bharat Mandapam

ITPO (India Trade Promotion Organisation) — India's premier trade promotion agency; HQ Pragati Maidan, New Delhi; regional offices Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Mumbai. Organises international trade fairs, buyer-seller meets, overseas market surveys.

Bharat Mandapam (International Exhibition-cum-Convention Centre) — at Pragati Maidan, New Delhi; inaugurated 2023; hosts up to 7,000 persons in a single format. Has hosted the G20 Summit, India International Trade Fair, Bharat Mobility Global Expo 2024, and the AI Bharat Expo 2025.

ECGC & Export Promotion Councils

ECGC Ltd. (formerly Export Credit Guarantee Corporation) — set up 1957; provides credit-risk insurance to exporters against commercial/political non-payment risk; also administers the National Export Insurance Account (NEIA) Trust for strategic project exports; offers Export Factoring for MSMEs.

Export Promotion Councils (EPCs) — non-profit exporter organisations recognised as registering authorities under the FTP. Currently 31 EPCs covering sectors like Apparels, Handicrafts, Gems & Jewellery, Marine Products, Engineering, Pharma.

CRIT (Centre for Research on International Trade) — a think tank on global trade/investment issues, housed within IIFT.

Districts as Export Hub & India's Trade Agreements

Districts as Export Hubs Scheme — targets export promotion/manufacturing/jobs at grassroots level; State Export Promotion Committees (SEPCs) and District Export Promotion Committees (DEPCs) established nationwide; District Specific Export Action Plans (DEAPs) drafted for 570 districts; export-potential products identified in 734 districts.

India's Trade Agreements: India has signed 15 Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) and 6 Preferential Trade Agreements — latest being the India-EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA) and the UK CETA. Ongoing FTA negotiations: European Union, Australia (CECA), New Zealand, Oman, Chile, Peru, Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU). Review negotiations ongoing for India-Korea CEPA and India-ASEAN FTA.

Exam angle: Don't confuse the India-UK CETA (Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement, signed) with pending negotiations like the India-EU FTA — one is done, others are ongoing.
